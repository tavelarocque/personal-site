darkmpc

A dark pool its operator cannot see into.

Dark pools hide orders from the market but not from the operator, and operators have misused that view (Barclays and Credit Suisse paid $154.3 million in 2016). darkmpc removes it: traders secret-share their orders across three servers, which run the matching auction together with secure three-party computation. No single server sees any order, total or fill, not even which stocks are traded.

Secure as long as at most one of the three servers cheats: every multiplication and shuffle is checked, and a cheat is caught before anything is opened. Checked against a plain reference matcher with 96 tests, 27 planted bugs the suite must catch, and written proofs.

0.95 s1,000-order midpoint auction, secure against a cheating server, LAN · 17.1 s across three US regions
4.3 s100,000-order auction, semi-honest mode, LAN
566 vs 4,509communication rounds against the published three-party MPC dark pool, on comparable 1,000-order batches

About 5× faster end to end than that system on comparable 1,000-order batches, on different hardware and networks.
