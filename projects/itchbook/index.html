What the view shows

Each column is a quarter second and each row a one-cent price. A cell's brightness is the number of shares resting at that price, for the ten best prices on each side, so the dark band through the middle is the spread and the white line inside it is the mid price. Under the heatmap, the amber bars are shares traded in each quarter second: buyer-initiated above the line, seller-initiated below, and executions against hidden orders in grey along the bottom.
